# Testing Alternate Reality 2.0

## Install the Android Preview

Use `Alternate-Reality-2.0.0-preview.apk` supplied by the app owner. Android may ask you to allow installation from the app you used to open the APK. The app is named **Alternate Reality Preview** and installs beside the original app without replacing its history. Minimum Android version: Android 7 (API 24).

This first package is an **offline preview** because hosting is being prepared for later. Daily motivation, reminder controls, onboarding and navigation can be tested now. The AI button stays disabled and explains why. A later connected update will use a personal invitation provided by the app owner; never enter an OpenAI API key in the app.

## Check on a real phone

1. Complete onboarding; inspect Home, History and Settings with normal and large system text.
2. Read today's motivation. Enable its notification; grant permission. Set the time a few minutes ahead. Background the app and check the notification. Android may delay delivery to save battery.
3. Reopen and confirm the chosen time and switches persist. Turn off daily motivation and confirm the current-reality preference is unchanged.
4. After a connected update: join with your invitation, submit a non-sensitive sentence in every mode, regenerate, copy/share, and return with New Thought. Check history and swipe-delete a record. Clear History only when you intend to remove all saved entries.
5. Try Literal Negation with “She enjoys her job”, “I went to work”, “I didn't go to work” and “They have finished”. Check grammar, tense and meaning; model wording can vary.
6. Check Positive is hopeful without promising success; darker modes should remain creative interpretations, not predictions or harmful instructions.
7. Disconnect the internet, submit/regenerate, and confirm a helpful error appears with the previous result preserved. Reconnect and retry.
8. Test voice input: allow/deny the microphone, speak, stop and switch tabs. Typing must always remain available.
9. With both notifications enabled and a generated result, confirm daily motivation and the current reality coexist. Check next-day change, reboot and time-zone changes. Do not force-stop the app for this check: Android force-stop suppresses background alarms until reopening.

Report the phone model, Android version, steps, expected result and actual result. Avoid screenshots containing personal thoughts, invitation codes or keys. Contact the person who invited you; no automatic feedback upload is included.

## Scope

This pass does not claim overnight/reboot delivery or real-model quality was verified on every device. Browser background notifications are unavailable. Apple distribution requires a separately signed build and platform checks. Do not upload this private preview to a public app store.
